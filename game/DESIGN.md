# Pixel Safari Sanctuary

Portrait cozy collection/management game. Six lures and a 45-second safari outing: tap a creature, then tap the capture ring when its marker passes the gold center. Return with caught creatures to increase collection, populate pens and earn visitor revenue. Every outing banks its rewards exactly once. Three habitats unlock at levels 1/3/6; the game never sells captured creatures or consumes parents for breeding.

200 collectible species span common/uncommon/rare/endangered/extinct tiers. 34 fixed two-parent recipes include breeding-exclusive species, with later recipes depending on earlier rare/endangered/extinct generations. Eggs mature using offline wall time; nursery capacity is two. Six pens have five upgrade levels, distinct capacities and income bonuses. Empty spaces can be managed from each pen.

Offline progress: coins, XP, creatures, pen assignments, breeding, 7-day escalating UTC login streak, daily capture quest, personal statistics and local achievements. Clock rollback cannot claim a duplicate daily reward. Idle enclosure income is capped at eight hours and 250,000 banked coins. Captured-but-unbanked safari creatures are recovered when the game restarts.

Native services are routed through /root/Services. Main menu displays banner; completed safaris trigger round_finished; valuable rewarded offer finishes current nursery eggs only after a positive earned-reward callback. Web keeps offline play and disables unavailable ads. Production service IDs/backend remain human configuration work.

Future DLC: Moonlight Wetlands, a new habitat and 40 additional creatures with separate breeding branches; no DLC implementation is required for the first release.
