Pixel Safari Sanctuary
A little sanctuary. A world of curious creatures.

Pixel Safari Sanctuary is a cozy creature-collecting game about short expeditions and the little sanctuary waiting when you return. Take your lures into the wild, time a capture, and bring your newest companion back to an enclosure of its own.

THREE SELLING CARDS
One more curious face
Take a short safari and bring a new companion home. Every rarity has a place in your collection.

A sanctuary that grows with you
Improve your enclosures, collect income, and make room for a thriving creature community.

Discover the family tree
Pair specific parents to uncover creatures you cannot find on an ordinary outing.

TECHNICAL INFORMATION
Made with Godot 4.5.1 Compatibility. Portrait Android is primary; a Web build is provided where feasible. Local saves support offline core play. Android services have graceful Web fallbacks. Original code is Apache-2.0; source-asset/plugin notices are preserved. Google Play Games and production ads require the owner's final configuration.
