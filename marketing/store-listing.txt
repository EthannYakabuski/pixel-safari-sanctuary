Pixel Safari Sanctuary

SHORT DESCRIPTION (75/80 characters)
Meet 200 curious monsters. Master each capture. Make room for every friend.

LONG DESCRIPTION
Meet them. Nurture them. Make room.

A tiny horned face appears between the ferns. The gold window moves. One well-timed chain brings a new friend home.

Pixel Safari Sanctuary combines quick capture challenges with a sanctuary you can keep growing. Explore eight illustrated trails, from the warm Meadow Footpath to snowy Frostglass Pass and violet Starfall Hollow. Earn your way into deeper habitats through keeper levels and completed outings, meeting familiar common creatures first and rarer companions later.

Every capture is a little skill challenge. Choose a creature, land every consecutive hit inside gold, and return with one resident. Common chains need two hits; endangered chains need five. A miss always breaks the chain. Upgrade your explorer's satchel for more attempts, and try again: normal safaris are always free.

Duplicates become bonds. Repeated captures raise shared species levels and the income of every matching resident. Reach level 5 with both exact parents to begin one of 34 nursery recipes, including species never found in the wild. Your parents stay with you.

Build a home worth returning to. Open as many meadows as you can afford, upgrade their space, and plan resident combinations for variety, home-habitat themes and named friendship bonuses. Each meadow shows what it earns. Your field guide records all 200 species across five rarity tiers and explains their bonds and favorite companions.

Collect visitor income, follow daily field notes and return for a seven-day gift cycle. Core play works offline, saves on this device and earns up to eight hours of visitor income while you are away. Optional advertising requires a connection. Online service availability depends on the final Android configuration.
